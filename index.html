Step-by-Step Lifecycle

How It Works

The step-by-step pipeline of an educational credit evaluation and simulation run.

STEP 01

Enter Financial Info

Input monthly income, on-time and total repayment counts, and existing outstanding debt obligations.

STEP 02

Calculate Factor Scores

Rule brackets map income (40-100), repayment percentage (0-100), and debt level (30-100).

STEP 03

Apply Weightages

Each factor is multiplied by its defined weight: Income × 30%, Repayments × 50%, Debt × 20%.

STEP 04

Generate Score & Rating

Sum the weighted scores to produce a 0–100 scale score and assign an educational rating (GOOD/AVG/POOR).

STEP 05

Simulate Financial Changes

Modify parameters (e.g. taking a fresh loan, increasing repayments, or salary change) in the simulator.

STEP 06

Compare Old vs New

Observe exact point changes (↑/↓), factor contributions, and rating impact side-by-side.
