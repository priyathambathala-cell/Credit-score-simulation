Scoring Rules & Weightages

Mathematical definitions and boundary conditions for educational evaluation

🧮 Test in Calculator

Core Scoring Equation

Final Estimated Score = (Income Score × 0.30) + (Repayment Score × 0.50) + (Debt Score × 0.20)

The engine normalizes all factor outputs into a bounded 0–100 numerical score, which is then mapped to a project-defined credit rating.

💡
Why is Repayment History weighted highest at 50%?
Repayment history receives the highest weight because consistent repayment behavior is treated as the strongest factor in this educational model. A borrower's track record of honoring past obligations is historically the most critical indicator of future credit discipline.

1. Monthly Income Scoring Strategy

Assigned Weight: 30% (0.30)

Factor 1

Income represents earning capacity and cash flow availability. Higher earnings provide stronger buffer for servicing debt.

2. Repayment Performance Strategy

Assigned Weight: 50% (0.50) — Dominant Weight

Factor 2

Calculated dynamically as the percentage of on-time installments made against total installment records.

Repayment Score Formula:
Repayment Percentage = (On-Time Repayments / Total Repayments) × 100
The resulting percentage value directly serves as the factor score (0–100).
Example Scenario On-Time / Total Repayment Score Weighted Points (50%)
Standard Demo Profile 9 / 10 records 90% 90 × 0.50 = 45.0 pts
Perfect Track Record 10 / 10 records 100% 100 × 0.50 = 50.0 pts (Max)
Moderate Delinquency 7 / 10 records 70% 70 × 0.50 = 35.0 pts
High Delinquency 4 / 10 records 40% 40 × 0.50 = 20.0 pts

3. Existing Debt Obligations Strategy

Assigned Weight: 20% (0.20)

Factor 3

Existing debt measures leverage risk. Higher debt reduces disposable income for servicing new credit.
