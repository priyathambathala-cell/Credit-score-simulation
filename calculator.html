🔍 How is this calculated? (Viva Explanation)

  • 1
    Income Score: Your monthly income is converted into a score using predefined income ranges.
    80 × 30% = 24
  • 2
    Repayment Score: Repayment Score = (On-time / Total) × 100.
    90% × 50% = 45
  • 3
    Debt Score: Existing debt is mapped inversely into a bracket score.
    70 × 20% = 14
  • 4
    Final Score: Final Score = weighted income + weighted repayment + weighted debt.
    24 + 45 + 14 = 83
